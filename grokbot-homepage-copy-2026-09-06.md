## Row 3: "Car Finance Commission Claims" (old text begins "Car finance commission claims relate to hidden or unfair commission agreements...")

Car finance commission claims are about hidden or unfair commission deals between lenders and the car dealers or brokers who arranged your finance. If you took out car finance, such as hire purchase (HP) or personal contract purchase (PCP), you should have been told about any commission and how it affected your interest rate. If you weren't, you may have been mis-sold.
